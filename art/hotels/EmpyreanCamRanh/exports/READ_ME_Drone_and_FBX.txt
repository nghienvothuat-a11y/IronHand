EMPYREAN CAM RANH — EXTERIOR + 20 SECOND DRONE

Files
Empyrean_CamRanh_Exterior_Drone.fbx: exterior, site, planting, beach/sea context and baked camera animation. Image textures are embedded. FBX import may also unpack them into a .fbm folder.
../source/Empyrean_CamRanh_Drone_20s.blend: editable animation and full render materials, lighting and original static cameras.
../renders/Empyrean_CamRanh_Drone_20s.mp4: Full HD, 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, exactly 20 seconds, H.264. No audio.

Route (one continuous shot)
0–3.8 s: From the sea toward the tower on the right, looking toward land.
3.8–6.5 s: Across the swimming pool, rising toward the curved glass facade.
6.5–9.5 s: Close glide along the window and balcony facade.
9.5–12.7 s: Swing around the wing toward the central east–west road.
12.7–16.5 s: Traverse the central road to the landward entrance, turn gaze back toward the resort.
16.5–20 s: Climb and pull away, holding the resort in view.
Travel speed increases on long segments to fit the complete route into 20 seconds.

Blender
Scene: Empyrean_Exterior. Animated camera: 08_Drone_20s.
Frames 1–480 at 24 fps. Press Numpad 0 for the camera; use the timeline to preview.
Final sequence uses EEVEE, 48 samples, screen reflections, indirect lighting and motion blur.
The static source Empyrean_CamRanh_Exterior.blend is retained separately.

FBX import
Units: metres. FBX axes: Y up, -Z forward; Blender import restores Z-up coordinates.
Use the baked animation/take at 24 fps. Blender FBX import: animation offset 0.
Material assignments and camera positions are checked by exporting and reimporting.
FBX carries portable materials. Procedural water/foliage shading, atmosphere, lights and render settings remain in the .blend file; another renderer may need material tuning.

Asset scope
Exterior reconstruction from photographs, with approximate dimensions; no interior rooms.
Four individual towers and the corrected outward-curving pavilion are retained.
